Stock Sentiment brings together real-time price data, historical charts, and AI-powered
sentiment analysis of financial news — all in one place.

## What you can do here

- **Search any stock** by ticker or company name to see live price, charts, and recent news
- **Track sentiment** as our AI classifies news articles about a company as positive, negative,
  or neutral
- **Watch trending stocks** across StockTwits, Reddit, and trading volume
- **Read the daily market wrap**, an automated end-of-day summary of how the major indices moved
  and why

We'll be publishing regular updates here on how the product is evolving. Thanks for stopping by.
